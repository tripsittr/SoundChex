## Option 1 — AGPLv3 (open source, free)

The default. The full text is in [LICENSE](LICENSE). In short, you may run,
study, modify and share SoundChex, **provided that**:

- if you distribute it (modified or not), you pass on the same freedoms and make
  the corresponding source available; and
- **because it is the *Affero* GPL**, if you run a modified version and let other
  people interact with it **over a network**, you must offer *those users* the
  complete corresponding source of your modified version (AGPLv3 §13).

For the overwhelming majority — self-hosters running SoundChex for themselves or
their household — this costs nothing and requires nothing further.

## Option 2 — Commercial license

The AGPLv3 does not suit everyone. You need a commercial license if you want to,
for example:

- **embed SoundChex in a closed-source product** you distribute;
- **offer SoundChex (or a derivative) as a hosted/SaaS service** without
  releasing your modifications' source under the AGPLv3; or
- otherwise use SoundChex in a way the AGPLv3's copyleft or §13 network clause
  does not permit.

A commercial license removes the AGPLv3 obligations for your use, under terms
agreed with Tripsittr LLC. See [COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md](COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md) for
the shape of the agreement.

**To obtain a commercial license, contact:** licensing@soundchex.app

## Third-party dependencies

The commercial license covers **SoundChex's own code**. The third-party libraries
it uses keep their own licenses (all permissive / AGPL-compatible — see
[Documentation & Planning/LicenseAudit.md](Documentation%20%26%20Planning/LicenseAudit.md)),
and you use those under their respective terms in either option. A commercial
license from Tripsittr LLC does not, and cannot, relicense third-party code.

contributed code in **both** the open-source and commercial licenses, while you
