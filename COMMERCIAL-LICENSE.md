# SoundChex Commercial License (draft)

> **This is a template describing the shape of a SoundChex commercial license, not
> a binding offer or contract.** The actual license is a signed agreement between

> obtain a commercial license, contact **licensing@soundchex.app**.

## Why this exists

SoundChex is available for free under the [AGPLv3](LICENSE). The AGPLv3's copyleft
and its network-use clause (§13) do not suit every use — for example, embedding
SoundChex in a closed-source product, or running a hosted service without
publishing your modifications. A commercial license removes those obligations for
your use.

See [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md) for which license you need.

## What a commercial license typically grants

Subject to the signed agreement and payment of the applicable fee, Tripsittr LLC
grants the licensee a license to use, modify and distribute **SoundChex's own
code** as part of the licensee's product or service **without** the AGPLv3's
source-disclosure and network-use obligations.

## What it does not cover

- **Third-party dependencies.** SoundChex's own code is licensed to you; the
  bundled/used third-party libraries keep their own (permissive) licenses and you
  use them under those terms. A commercial license cannot relicense code Tripsittr
  LLC does not own. See
  [Documentation & Planning/LicenseAudit.md](Documentation%20%26%20Planning/LicenseAudit.md).
- **Trademarks.** The "SoundChex" name and logo are trademarks of Tripsittr LLC
  and are not licensed for your product's branding except as separately agreed.

## Typical terms (to be set in the signed agreement)

These are the dimensions a commercial agreement usually fixes — the actual values
are negotiated and set in the contract, not here:

- **Scope** — which products/services, and which use (embed, host, redistribute).
- **Term** — perpetual for a version, or a subscription with updates.
- **Fee** — one-time and/or recurring.
- **Support & updates** — whether included, and at what level.
- **Warranty & liability** — as negotiated (the AGPLv3 "as is" default may be
  varied by the commercial agreement).

## How to obtain one

Email **licensing@soundchex.app** with a short description of your intended use
(embedding vs. hosting, scale, whether you'll distribute). We'll take it from
there.
