## Worth knowing

**This is a draft template for legal review, not legal advice and not a binding
agreement.** It is deliberately structured the way commercial-OSS licences are
(alternative-to-the-copyleft grant, third-party carve-out, trademark carve-out)
so counsel starts from a real document rather than a blank page. A commercial
licence exists only once a lawyer-reviewed version is signed by both parties.
